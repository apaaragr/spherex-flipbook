Chapter 1 · The Milky Way

You are here.

Our galaxy is about 100,000 light-years across, a slowly turning disc of a few hundred billion stars. The Sun is one of them, about 26,000 light-years from the centre.

The galaxy's shape is an artist's impression: no telescope can photograph the Milky Way from outside.

Chapter 2 · The Sun's corner

A minor arm called the Orion Spur.

We live between two of the galaxy's great spiral arms. Everything the challenge asked us to find sits in this tiny corner, so close that at galaxy scale it would all fit inside the Sun's dot.

Chapter 3 · Our neighbourhood

Pick a star.

Each star sits in its true direction from the Sun. Distances run from 6 to 2,600 light-years, so they're drawn on a logarithmic scale: each ring is ten times farther than the one inside it.
